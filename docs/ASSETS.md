# Bild- und Klangquellen

`assets/worlds.webp`: für dieses Projekt mit dem eingebauten ImageGen-Werkzeug erzeugter Atlas, anschließend verlustarm in WebP gespeichert. Der Renderer wählt die Quadranten zur Laufzeit; es gibt keine externe Bild-URL.

## Erzeugungsprompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a production game background atlas for an original premium painterly dark fairytale RPG 'Nebelpfad'. One square image divided into EXACTLY FOUR equal square panels in a clean 2x2 grid, no gaps, no borders, no writing, no labels, no UI, NO CHARACTERS. Each panel is a detailed bird's eye / near-topdown orthographic environment with consistent scale, usable as a traversable mobile game room. Top-left: autumn forest clearing at dusk, ochre stone footpath winding from lower center to upper center, old stone arch in upper center, amber campfire clearing lower left, mossy rocks, dense trees around EDGES, open center for movement. Top-right: abandoned medieval hamlet square at blue hour, cobbled OPEN central square, small timber cottages at corners and edges, old well on right side, warm windows, small ruined chapel upper center. Bottom-left: subterranean archive circular hall, teal dark stone, broken masonry at perimeter, ancient bookshelves around edges, broad open stone central floor, amber lantern sconces, large doorway upper center. Bottom-right: ancient forest sanctuary, huge circular stone ritual dais occupying central floor, three subtle carved concentric circles, blue luminescent roots and gold lamps around edges, misty gulf beyond, stairs at bottom. Hand painted rich detailed environmental concept art, soft volumetric lighting, sophisticated muted emerald, blue slate, moss and warm gold palette, beautiful tactile painterly textures, strong compositional clarity. Camera looks steeply downward, almost topdown so the floor fills each panel; NO horizon, NO sky. Keep walking areas open. Each panel coherent standalone square environment. High quality game art.

Figuren, Licht und Partikel: eigener Canvas-Code in `world.mjs`. Icons: eigene SVG-Grafik; Oberflächensymbole sind Unicode-Zeichen. Schriften: Systemschrift und Georgia, kein CDN. Klang: synthetische Web-Audio-Töne; keine Fremdaufnahmen. Atmosphärenklang standardmäßig aus und nur nach Benutzertipp aktiv.
